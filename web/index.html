01

What is TrustRAG for?

TrustRAG is a research assistant for asking questions about your own PDF sources. It retrieves relevant passages, drafts a short answer from that evidence, and points you to the source page. It is designed to make checking an answer easier—not to replace reading, expert judgment, or reliable original sources.

Good fit

Compare policies, locate a requirement in a manual, review a research paper, or find the exact passage behind a claim.

02

Quick start: from PDF to answer

  1. Create an accountUse an email address and a strong password. Your library belongs to your account.
  2. Upload a searchable PDFOpen My library and choose a text-based PDF under 20 MB and 500 pages, then select Upload and index. Each account can index up to 100 documents.
  3. Ask a precise questionOpen Workspace. Name a topic, requirement, or detail you want to find in your sources.
  4. Inspect the evidenceOpen the evidence cards. Check the quotation, document name, and page against the original PDF.
  5. Remove documents when finishedDelete sources you no longer need from My library.
03

How TrustRAG creates an answer

01 · Extract

Readable text is extracted from PDF pages.

02 · Retrieve

Semantic search finds the passages most relevant to your question.

03 · Draft

A configured answer model receives your question and those passages.

04 · Cite

TrustRAG keeps sentences with recognized evidence IDs and displays their source pages.

The hosted AI provider may process retrieved document passages to generate an answer. These passages are sent only when you submit a question, not when you merely browse the site or upload a PDF.

04

Privacy and your documents

  • Sign-in is required before document upload, search, and answering.
  • Every document query is filtered by the signed-in account.
  • The server stores extracted page text and vectors so your documents can be searched.
  • The configured hosted AI provider receives relevant passages when you request a generated answer.
  • Review the deployment's provider and privacy policy before uploading confidential or regulated records.
  • Use the library's Delete action to remove a document and its searchable passages.
  • Use Delete account in the header to permanently remove your account, sessions, and indexed documents.
  • Service limits: 20 MB per PDF, 500 pages per PDF, 100 PDFs per account, and 5,000 total passages per account.
05

Accuracy, evidence, and honest limits

No AI system can promise 100% accuracy.

A citation proves that the cited passage was retrieved—not that it fully supports every claim. Models can misunderstand context, omit qualifications, or make mistakes. Inspect important claims in the original page and confirm them independently.

  • TrustRAG can abstain when evidence is too weak; a result is not guaranteed for every question.
  • Scanned/image-only PDFs need OCR before they can be searched.
  • Conflicting passages are shown for your review; conflicts are not automatically resolved.
  • Account passwords cannot be reset automatically in this release. Store yours safely.
  • This is a portfolio application, not a regulated medical, legal, financial, or enterprise records service.
06

Troubleshooting

A PDF uploads but no text is found

It may be scanned or image-only. Use OCR to create a text-searchable PDF and upload it again.

The answer says evidence is insufficient

Upload a source that directly addresses the question, confirm indexing completed, and ask a narrower question using the document's terminology.

The answer takes a long time or fails

The service can be busy, a provider may be unavailable, or the administrator may not have configured the answer service. Retry once; share the exact error with the site administrator.

I cannot sign in

Check the email address and password. Password reset email is not configured; if you lose your password, contact the site owner.

A cited answer looks wrong

Open its evidence card and compare it with the original cited page. Do not rely on an unsupported or misleading answer for a consequential decision.

CLEAR BY DESIGN

Privacy & data use

Know what is stored, what is sent for answer generation, and how to remove your sources.

Account library

Uploaded documents are extracted and indexed on the TrustRAG server. Account scoping is applied to read, search, ask, and delete operations. A hosting administrator can access server infrastructure.

Answer generation

When you submit a question, TrustRAG sends your question and retrieved passages to the administrator-configured AI provider. Read that provider's privacy/retention terms before uploading sensitive content.

Deletion

Delete an indexed source in My library to remove its stored passages and vectors. Delete account in the header to remove the account, sessions, and its documents. Backups and provider retention policies depend on the deployment and provider.

Your responsibility

Upload only materials you are authorized to process. Do not upload secrets, regulated data, or sensitive personal records unless the site operator has explicitly approved that use.
