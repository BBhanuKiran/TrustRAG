01

What is TrustRAG for?

TrustRAG is an evidence-first research assistant for asking questions across your PDFs, text, images, web pages, and current web search results. It retrieves relevant passages, drafts a short answer from that evidence, and links you to its source. It helps make checking easier—not replace reading, expert judgment, or reliable original sources.

Good fit

Compare policies, locate a requirement, inspect a chart with readable labels, review research, or check recent information against your own sources.

02

Quick start: from sources to answers

  1. Create an accountUse an email address and a strong password. Your library belongs to your account.
  2. Add source materialUpload PDFs, text files, or images; paste text; or add a public web link. Images are converted to text using local Tesseract OCR.
  3. Ask a precise questionCurrent web results are included by default. Uncheck “Include fresh web results” to search only your private sources.
  4. Inspect the evidenceOpen evidence cards, check quoted text and citations, and follow source links. PDF citations include page numbers.
  5. Remove sources when finishedDelete sources you no longer need from My library.
03

How TrustRAG creates an answer

01 · Collect

Extract text from files, images, and links; search the web when enabled.

02 · Retrieve

Semantic search finds passages most relevant to your question.

03 · Draft

A configured answer model receives your question and selected passages.

04 · Cite

TrustRAG validates evidence IDs and provides page references or source links.

Web searches send your question to DuckDuckGo. If you use a hosted answer provider, it receives the question and retrieved excerpts. These providers may process your data under their own terms.

04

Privacy and your sources

  • Sign-in is required before adding, searching, or answering from private sources.
  • Every private-library query is filtered by the signed-in account.
  • The server stores extracted text and vectors so private sources can be searched.
  • When enabled, web search sends your question to DuckDuckGo; a hosted AI provider may receive retrieved excerpts when you request an answer.
  • Image OCR runs locally. Uploaded images are not sent to an AI vision service.
  • Review provider privacy terms before using confidential or regulated material.
  • Use Delete to remove a source and its searchable passages. Delete account removes all of your indexed sources.
  • Limits: 20 MB per file/link, 500 pages per PDF, 100 sources per account, and 5,000 passages per account.
05

Accuracy, evidence, and honest limits

No AI system can promise 100% accuracy.

Search results come from DuckDuckGo and may be incomplete, outdated, or unavailable. A citation proves that text was retrieved—not that it fully supports every claim. Models can misunderstand context or make mistakes. Inspect important claims in the original source.

  • Search engines do not guarantee the newest or complete information; check source publication dates.
  • OCR can misread images, handwriting, small print, or complex layouts. It extracts text, not visual meaning.
  • TrustRAG can abstain when evidence is too weak; a result is not guaranteed for every question.
  • Conflicting passages are shown for review; conflicts are not automatically resolved.
  • This is a portfolio application, not a regulated medical, legal, financial, or enterprise records service.
06

Troubleshooting

A PDF uploads but no text is found

It may be scanned or image-only. Use OCR to create a text-searchable PDF and upload it again.

Image indexing says Tesseract is missing

Install Tesseract OCR and add its executable to PATH, then restart TrustRAG. Image OCR reads printed text; it may not read handwriting or understand charts and diagrams.

A web link cannot be added

Use a direct public HTTP or HTTPS link. Private-network links and pages that redirect are blocked; open the final page and add its direct address.

Fresh web search fails

DuckDuckGo may be unavailable or rate-limited. Retry later or uncheck Include fresh web results to use only your private library.

The answer says evidence is insufficient

Add a source that directly addresses the question, confirm indexing completed, and ask a narrower question using the source's terminology.

The answer takes a long time or fails

The service can be busy, a provider may be unavailable, or the administrator may not have configured the answer service. Retry once; share the exact error with the site administrator.

I cannot sign in

Check the email address and password. Password reset email is not configured; if you lose your password, contact the site owner.

A cited answer looks wrong

Open its evidence card and compare it with the original source. Do not rely on an unsupported or misleading answer for a consequential decision.

CLEAR BY DESIGN

Privacy & data use

Know what is stored, what is sent for answer generation, and how to remove your sources.

Account library

Uploaded documents are extracted and indexed on the TrustRAG server. Account scoping is applied to read, search, ask, and delete operations. A hosting administrator can access server infrastructure.

Answer generation

When you submit a question, TrustRAG sends your question and retrieved passages to the administrator-configured AI provider. Read that provider's privacy/retention terms before uploading sensitive content.

Deletion

Delete an indexed source in My library to remove its stored passages and vectors. Delete account in the header to remove the account, sessions, and its documents. Backups and provider retention policies depend on the deployment and provider.

Your responsibility

Upload only materials you are authorized to process. Do not upload secrets, regulated data, or sensitive personal records unless the site operator has explicitly approved that use.
